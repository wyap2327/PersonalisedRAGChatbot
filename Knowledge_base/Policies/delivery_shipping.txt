DELIVERY AND SHIPPING POLICY — SHOPNEST E-COMMERCE STORE
Last Updated: January 2026

=============================================================
SECTION 1: UK DELIVERY OPTIONS
=============================================================

ShopNest offers the following delivery options for all UK orders:

1. Standard Delivery
   - Cost: £3.99 per order
   - FREE on all orders over £50
   - Estimated delivery: 3–5 business days from dispatch
   - Available: Monday to Saturday (excluding bank holidays)
   - Courier: Royal Mail Tracked 48 or Evri (formerly Hermes), depending on parcel size

2. Express Delivery
   - Cost: £6.99 per order
   - Estimated delivery: 1–2 business days from dispatch
   - Available: Monday to Friday
   - Orders placed before 2pm (GMT) on a business day are dispatched the same day
   - Orders placed after 2pm or on weekends are dispatched the next business day
   - Courier: DPD or DHL Express

3. Next Day Delivery
   - Cost: £9.99 per order
   - Guaranteed next business day delivery when ordered before 11pm (GMT)
   - Available Monday to Thursday (orders placed Friday will arrive Monday)
   - Signature required on delivery
   - Courier: DPD Next Day

4. Click and Collect
   - Cost: FREE
   - Available at over 3,000 collection points across the UK (including selected Co-op, Collect+, and Doddle locations)
   - Orders are ready for collection within 2–4 business days
   - Items must be collected within 10 days or they will be returned and a refund issued

=============================================================
SECTION 2: FREE DELIVERY THRESHOLD
=============================================================

Standard delivery is FREE for all UK orders with a basket value of £50 or more (after any discounts are applied). Free delivery is automatically applied at checkout — no code is required. Free delivery does not apply to express or next day delivery options.

=============================================================
SECTION 3: DISPATCH TIMES
=============================================================

- Orders placed Monday to Friday before 2pm (GMT) are dispatched the same business day.
- Orders placed after 2pm or on weekends/bank holidays are dispatched the next business day.
- During peak periods (e.g., Black Friday, Christmas), dispatch times may be extended by 1–2 additional business days. Any delays will be communicated via email.
- Pre-order items will be dispatched on the stated release date.

=============================================================
SECTION 4: INTERNATIONAL DELIVERY
=============================================================

ShopNest currently ships to the following regions:

European Union (EU)
   - Cost: £12.99 per order
   - Estimated delivery: 5–10 business days from dispatch
   - Note: Customers are responsible for any import duties, VAT, or customs fees applicable in their country. These are not included in our delivery charge.
   - Courier: DHL International or Royal Mail International Tracked

United States and Canada
   - Cost: £19.99 per order
   - Estimated delivery: 7–14 business days from dispatch
   - Import duties and taxes are the responsibility of the recipient
   - Courier: FedEx International

Australia and New Zealand
   - Cost: £22.99 per order
   - Estimated delivery: 10–18 business days from dispatch
   - Import duties and taxes are the responsibility of the recipient

Rest of World
   - International shipping is not available to all countries. Please check the checkout for availability for your country.
   - Estimated delivery: 14–21 business days
   - Cost: £24.99

Important Note on International Orders:
ShopNest is not responsible for delays caused by customs processing. We are unable to falsify customs declarations or mark goods as gifts to avoid import fees — this is illegal and we do not comply with such requests.

=============================================================
SECTION 5: TRACKING YOUR ORDER
=============================================================

- A shipping confirmation email containing your tracking number is sent once your order is dispatched.
- Tracking is available via the courier's website or via "My Orders" in your ShopNest account.
- Live tracking updates are provided by DPD and DHL. Royal Mail and Evri provide milestone tracking (e.g., dispatched, out for delivery, delivered).
- If your tracking has not updated within 5 business days of dispatch, please contact our support team.

=============================================================
SECTION 6: FAILED DELIVERIES
=============================================================

- If no one is available at the delivery address, the courier will leave a delivery attempt card and typically retry delivery the next business day.
- After 2 failed delivery attempts, parcels are held at a local depot or collection point for 7 days before being returned to ShopNest.
- If a parcel is returned due to failed delivery, we will contact you to arrange re-delivery (an additional delivery charge of £3.99 applies) or issue a refund minus the original delivery cost.

=============================================================
SECTION 7: LOST OR DAMAGED PARCELS
=============================================================

Lost Parcels:
- A parcel is considered lost if it has not been delivered within 10 business days of the expected delivery date (UK) or 28 business days (international).
- Contact our support team who will raise a formal investigation with the courier. Investigations typically take 5–10 business days.
- If confirmed lost, we will dispatch a replacement order at no cost or issue a full refund including original delivery charges.

Damaged Parcels:
- If your order arrives damaged, take photographs of both the packaging and the item before opening fully.
- Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and your order number.
- We will arrange a free return and offer a replacement or full refund.

=============================================================
SECTION 8: DELIVERY RESTRICTIONS
=============================================================

- We do not deliver to PO Boxes for express or next day delivery. Standard delivery to PO Boxes is available via Royal Mail.
- Some large or heavy items (e.g., items over 30kg) may incur an additional delivery surcharge, which will be shown at checkout.
- Certain products (e.g., aerosols, batteries over a certain capacity) are restricted from air freight and cannot be shipped internationally.
