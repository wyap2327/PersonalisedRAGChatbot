RETURNS AND REFUNDS POLICY — SHOPNEST E-COMMERCE STORE
Last Updated: January 2026

=============================================================
SECTION 1: RETURN WINDOW
=============================================================

You can return most items within 30 days of the date of delivery for a full refund or exchange. This is in addition to your statutory rights under UK law.

For items purchased during the Christmas period (1st November to 31st December), the return window is extended to 60 days from the date of delivery to accommodate gift purchases.

Under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 (distance selling rules), you also have the legal right to cancel your order within 14 days of receiving it, even if the item is not faulty. You then have a further 14 days to return the item after notifying us. Our 30-day policy is more generous than this legal minimum.

=============================================================
SECTION 2: ITEMS YOU CAN RETURN
=============================================================

To be eligible for a return, your item must meet all of the following conditions:
- Returned within the applicable return window (30 days standard, 60 days Christmas)
- In original, unused condition with all tags, labels, and accessories intact
- In its original packaging
- Accompanied by your order number or proof of purchase

You are entitled to handle an item in the same way you would in a shop — for example, trying on clothing or taking a product out of its box to inspect it. However, if an item has been used beyond reasonable inspection, we may reduce your refund to account for any loss in value.

=============================================================
SECTION 3: ITEMS THAT CANNOT BE RETURNED
=============================================================

The following items can only be returned if they are faulty or not as described:

- Perishable goods (e.g., food items, coffee beans once the seal has been broken)
- Personalised or custom-made items (e.g., engraved products, made-to-measure orders)
- Sealed items that cannot be returned for hygiene or health reasons once opened (e.g., underwear, swimwear, earphones with used ear tips)
- Unwrapped or downloaded digital content (e.g., software, digital game codes once redeemed)
- Newspapers and magazines
- Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable" at the time of purchase

If any of the above items arrive damaged, faulty, or not as described, you are still entitled to a repair, replacement, or refund. Please contact our support team within 14 days of delivery.

=============================================================
SECTION 4: HOW TO RETURN AN ITEM
=============================================================

Step 1 — Request Your Return Online:
Log in to your ShopNest account, go to "My Orders", find the order containing the item you want to return, click "Return Item(s)", select the item(s), choose your reason for returning, and confirm. If you checked out as a guest, email us at support@shopnest.co.uk with your order number and we will start the process for you.

Step 2 — Receive Your Prepaid Return Label:
Once your return request is approved, you will receive a prepaid Royal Mail or Evri return label by email within 1 hour. Print and attach it securely to your parcel.

Step 3 — Drop Off Your Parcel:
Take your parcel to any Royal Mail Post Office or Evri drop-off point (over 7,000 locations across the UK). Keep your drop-off receipt as proof of postage until your refund is confirmed.

Step 4 — We Process Your Return:
Once we receive your item and confirm it meets the return conditions, we will process your refund within 3–5 business days. You will receive a confirmation email when the refund has been issued.

=============================================================
SECTION 5: RETURN COSTS
=============================================================

- Change of mind returns: Return postage is FREE using our prepaid label.
- Faulty, damaged, or incorrect items: Return postage is FREE and we will cover any re-delivery costs for your replacement.
- International returns: You are responsible for the cost of return postage. We recommend using a tracked service. We will not reimburse international return postage unless the item is confirmed faulty.

=============================================================
SECTION 6: REFUNDS — HOW AND WHEN YOU WILL BE PAID BACK
=============================================================

All refunds are returned to your original payment method. Timelines are as follows:

- Credit or Debit Card: Refund issued within 3–5 business days of return confirmation. Allow a further 3–5 business days for your bank to process it.
- PayPal: Refund issued within 3–5 business days and appears in your PayPal balance immediately once processed.
- Apple Pay / Google Pay: Refund returned to the linked card within 3–5 business days.
- ShopNest Gift Card: Credit returned to your Gift Card balance within 1–2 business days.
- Klarna (Buy Now Pay Later): Applied to your outstanding Klarna balance. If you have already paid in full, Klarna will refund your original payment method within 5–7 business days.
- Loyalty Points: Any points redeemed on your order will be reinstated to your account within 5 business days of the return being processed.

Delivery charges: If you cancel your order under the 14-day cooling-off period (distance selling rights), we will refund the cost of standard delivery. If you paid for express or next day delivery, we will only refund the equivalent standard delivery cost — not the upgraded delivery fee.

=============================================================
SECTION 7: FAULTY OR DAMAGED ITEMS
=============================================================

If your item arrives damaged or develops a fault, your rights depend on when the problem occurs:

Within 30 days of delivery:
You have the right to a full refund, a repair, or a replacement. Contact our support team with your order number and photos of the fault and we will resolve it promptly.

Within 6 months of delivery:
If a fault develops within 6 months of purchase, it is assumed the item was faulty when sold, unless we can prove otherwise. We will offer a repair or replacement first. If the repair or replacement fails, you are entitled to a full or partial refund.

After 6 months and up to 6 years (5 years in Scotland):
You may still be entitled to a repair, replacement, or partial refund, but you will need to provide evidence that the fault existed at the time of purchase. Claims are assessed on a case-by-case basis.

To report a faulty item, contact us at support@shopnest.co.uk or via live chat with your order number, a description of the fault, and photos where possible.

=============================================================
SECTION 8: EXCHANGES
=============================================================

ShopNest does not currently offer direct exchanges. If you would like a different size, colour, or product, please return the original item for a full refund and place a new order. This ensures the quickest resolution and guarantees availability of the item you want.

=============================================================
SECTION 9: ITEMS YOU DID NOT BUY — GIFTS AND THIRD-PARTY PURCHASES
=============================================================

Returns can only be accepted from the person who made the original purchase. If you received an item as a gift and wish to return it, the original buyer will need to initiate the return. The refund will be issued to the original payment method. If this is a problem, please contact our support team and we will try to assist.

=============================================================
SECTION 10: PROOF OF PURCHASE
=============================================================

We may ask you to provide proof that you purchased the item from ShopNest. Acceptable proof includes your order confirmation email, your order number, a bank or PayPal statement showing the transaction, or the original packaging with ShopNest branding.

=============================================================
SECTION 11: WARRANTIES AND GUARANTEES
=============================================================

A manufacturer's warranty or guarantee does not affect your statutory rights. Even if your warranty has expired, you may still be entitled to a repair or replacement under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 if the item develops a fault that would not be expected given its price and description.

If your item is still within its manufacturer's warranty period, we can also help you make a warranty claim on your behalf. Contact our support team with your order details and a description of the fault.
