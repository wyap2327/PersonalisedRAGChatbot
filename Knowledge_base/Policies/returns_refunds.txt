RETURNS AND REFUNDS POLICY — SHOPNEST E-COMMERCE STORE

Q: What is the return policy?
A: You can return most ShopNest items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Here's what you need to know:
    - Items must be unworn, unwashed, and still have their original tags and packaging intact.
    - Proof of purchase (order number or confirmation email) is required.
    - Returns are free for all ShopNest customers using our prepaid label.

Q: Can I return more than one order in the same parcel?
A: No. Please return each order separately and include any packaging that forms part of the goods with your valid proof of purchase.

Q: How do I return an item?
A: Log in to your account, go to "My Orders", find the relevant order, and click "Return Item(s)". Select the item(s) and your reason for returning. You will receive a prepaid Royal Mail or Evri return label by email within 1 hour. Print it, attach it to your parcel, and drop it off at any Royal Mail Post Office or Evri drop-off point. Keep your drop-off receipt until your refund is confirmed.

Q: Where is my refund?
A: Once we receive your returned item and confirm it meets our return conditions, we will process your refund within 3-5 business days. You will receive a confirmation email when this is done. Allow a further 3-5 business days for the refund to appear in your account depending on your bank. Refunds are always returned to your original payment method.

Q: Can I exchange an item for a different size or colour?
A: ShopNest does not currently offer direct exchanges. Please return the original item for a full refund and place a new order for the size or colour you need. This guarantees availability and gives you the fastest resolution.

Q: What if my item arrives faulty or damaged?
A: If your item arrives damaged or develops a fault within 30 days of delivery, you are entitled to a full refund, repair, or replacement. Contact our customer support team at support@shopnest.co.uk with your order number, a description of the fault, and photos where possible. We will resolve it promptly at no cost to you.

Q: How much is return cost?
A: 
- Change of mind returns: Return postage is FREE using our prepaid label.
- Faulty, damaged, or incorrect items: Return postage is FREE and we will cover any re-delivery costs for your replacement.

Q: ITEMS YOU DID NOT BUY — GIFTS AND THIRD-PARTY PURCHASES
A: Returns can only be accepted from the person who made the original purchase. If you received an item as a gift and wish to return it, the original buyer will need to initiate the return. The refund will be issued to the original payment method. If this is a problem, please contact our support team and we will try to assist.

Q: PROOF OF PURCHASE
A: We may ask you to provide proof that you purchased the item from ShopNest. Acceptable proof includes your order confirmation email, your order number, a bank or PayPal statement showing the transaction, or the original packaging with ShopNest branding.
